## Core Facts

- Ashcombe's death is an accident, not a murder: Benedict Hale casts a
  nonlethal restraining spell during a confrontation with Ashcombe; it
  strikes unstable Founder-era masonry, which collapses on Ashcombe.
  Benedict does not intend to kill him, but directly causes the death,
  then conceals his role until confronted.
- Benedict never lies. He evades, deflects, and stays silent, but never
  states a falsehood outright.
- The mystery is about understanding, not solving a murder — the case is
  fully solved by Chapter 36 of 45; the remaining chapters are about the
  moral weight of what was found, not who did it.
